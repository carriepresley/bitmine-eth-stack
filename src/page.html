The stack

BitMine bought 4.9× more ETH than the network added

When BitMine announced its ETH treasury on June 30, 2025, there were 120,868,355 ETH. Since then the supply has grown by a net 1,213,517, while BitMine disclosed 6,001,302. In 59 of the 65 intervals between its releases it added more ETH than the entire network did. It fell short only in the weeks ending Jul 19, Jul 26, Aug 2, Aug 9, Aug 16 and Sep 27, 2026; it slowed ETH buying in July and August 2026 while it bought back its own stock.

Created vs. burned

Staking mints about 30 ETH for every 1 that gets burned

ETH supply changes with every block. Validators earn newly minted ETH as staking rewards, and every transaction destroys its base fee. Fees have stayed low, so the burn is small, and supply has grown by about 18,670 ETH a week since BitMine started buying.

All ETH, ever

Where all 122 million ETH came from

About 72 million ETH existed at launch in 2015. Proof-of-work miners earned 50.4 million more before the Merge in September 2022, and proof-of-stake has paid 4.35 million in rewards since December 2020. EIP-1559 has burned 4.63 million since August 2021. BitMine's 6.00 million is more than all the ETH ever burned and more than every staking reward ever paid.

Method

  • One dot or block is 10,000 ETH; counts are rounded.
  • Holdings are BitMine's disclosed totals at each release's stated "as of" time.
  • Share of supply divides holdings by on-chain supply on the same date. BitMine's releases used 120.7 million as the denominator until September 2026, so their stated percentages run slightly higher.
  • Burned ETH is derived each day as new issuance minus the change in total supply.
  • Supply figures from different providers differ by a few thousand ETH, depending on how they count pending validator deposits.
