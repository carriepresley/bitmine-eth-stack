Method

  • One dot or block is 10,000 ETH; counts are rounded.
  • Holdings are BitMine's disclosed totals at each release's stated "as of" time.
  • Share of all ETH divides holdings by on-chain supply on the same date. BitMine's releases used 120.7 million as the denominator until September 2026, so their stated percentages run slightly higher.
  • New ETH is daily issuance to stakers. Burned ETH is issuance minus the change in total supply.
  • Staking rewards are estimated as staked ETH × BitMine's latest disclosed 7-day yield.
